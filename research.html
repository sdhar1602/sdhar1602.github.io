02 · Research

Research.

I study energy systems, from electricity markets and demand forecasting to sustainability, alongside interpretable AI with an emphasis on distillation. More broadly, I build data-driven models to understand complex systems and inform decision-making.

Research themes

01

Energy & electricity markets

Electricity markets, demand forecasting and sustainability, including a methodology for long-term hourly demand forecasting driven by GDP and invariant load profiles.

02

Interpretable AI

Interpretability with an emphasis on distillation: using genetic programming to distil trained models into compact, human-readable symbolic expressions.

03

Data-driven decisions

Data-driven modeling of complex systems to inform decision-making, from energy systems to how generative AI reshapes collaboration in supply chain management.
